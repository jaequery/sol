Filmstrip Focus

Cinema-first and almost chromeless. There is no permanent inspector — the preview takes nearly the whole window and every control arrives as a popover anchored to what you clicked. The timeline is a literal filmstrip of frame thumbnails rather than coloured bars, so you scrub by looking at pictures. Keyframes hang under the strip with the prompt written on the segment itself. Best if the app should feel calm and content-first.
