Concept 5 of 5

Studio Bold

Opinionated and high-contrast: hard black borders, flat colour blocks, monospaced numerals, chunky keyframe diamonds you cannot mis-click. Same three-pane structure as Concept 1 but with none of the grey — every control announces itself, and state is carried by colour (lime = selected, coral = generating/record, lilac = AI). Best if the app should look like nothing else on the dock and be readable on a laptop screen in a café.
