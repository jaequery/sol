Prompt Console

The AI is the product, so the right rail is a generation console, not a properties panel: you compose a prompt against a keyframe pair, hit send, and every job lands as a card you can watch, retry, or discard — queued, running, failed and done all visible at once. The timeline shrinks to a compact strip because the interesting work happens in the console. Best if generation is expected to be slow, frequent, and worth managing.
