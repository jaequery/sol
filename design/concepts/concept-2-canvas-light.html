Canvas Light

A bright, Figma-flavoured take. The preview is the workspace — you drag the photo directly on an infinite dotted canvas to set each keyframe's framing, and the timeline floats over it as a card you can collapse. No media bin taking permanent space; keyframes sit on a rail beneath the clip so they never crowd the thumbnail. Best if the app should feel like a modern design tool rather than an NLE.
