# SolCut — UX audit and polish pass

**Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Base:** `9a63ffd` · **Method:** four specialist reviews (visual design,
usability/flow, accessibility, layout/CSS architecture) over 23 seeded app states at 1440×900
and the 1080×660 minimum, plus the user's own retina screenshot of the packaged macOS app.

The four reviews converged on the same handful of root causes, all fixed in this pass.

## What was wrong, and what changed

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two title bars on macOS: the native bar sat above the app's own 38px bar. | `tauri.conf.json`: `titleBarStyle: Overlay`, `hiddenTitle`, `trafficLightPosition` x14/y13. The app's bar pads 80px on the left under `data-platform="mac"` (set from `isDesktop()` + `navigator.platform`) so the lights never cover the title. **Needs an app restart to take effect** — config is read at launch. |
| 2 | "+ Import" / "✦ Generate" wrapped onto two lines: 224px column, ~214px of unbreakable content. | Bin is 280px; the head's title is the only thing allowed to ellipsize; actions are `nowrap`, `flex: 0 0 auto`. |
| 3 | `--ink-3` (#646b8a) failed AA on every surface (3.2–3.7:1); it carried every hint, label and section title. `.tc--dim` was 2.4:1. | `--ink-3: #7e85a3` (4.6:1 on `--panel-2`), `--ink-2` lifted to #a3a9c4; the transport's total reads `--ink-3`. |
| 4 | Text down to 8px; 22 one-off sizes bypassed the scale. | Scale is 10/11/12/13/14/16; 10px is reserved for badges beside larger text. |
| 5 | Targets under 24px everywhere: tools 22, chips 22, bin ✕ 18, mute 18, handles 10px wide. | Control-height tokens 24/28/32/36; every control uses one; trim handles are 14px. |
| 6 | Emoji (🗑 🔊 🔇 ✨ 🎞) mixed with dingbats and text glyphs across the toolbar, inspector and preview. | `src/components/Icon.tsx` — one stroke SVG set, `aria-hidden`, used everywhere. |
| 7 | Preview capped at 720px inside a ~924px column (~56% of the stage used). | `.stage` is a size container; `.canvas` is `min(100%, 100cqh × 16/9)` — fills whichever axis is tighter. |
| 8 | Cut chip centred on the clip seam covered ~60% of both neighbours' trim handles; at 26% zoom 22px chips outweighed 24px clips. | Chip rides the top seam of the cut, above the clip band; below 44px of clip on either side it becomes a 12px dot. |
| 9 | Two playhead elements (track, lanes), neither in the ruler; head buried in the track padding. | One overlay spanning ruler + track + lanes, head in the ruler. |
| 10 | Timeline height unbounded — six lanes at 660px crushed the preview to ~165px. | `.timeline__scroll` scrolls inside `max-height: 44vh`. |
| 11 | No keyboard path to scrub; no Home/End; Delete swallowed on a focused clip; Space suppressed button activation historically. | Global: Space, Home, End, ← → (Shift ×10), S = split, ⌫ = delete outside text fields. Ruler is a `role="slider"` with the same keys. A legend of the keys sits in the toolbar. |
| 12 | Modals had no focus trap or restore; textarea/inputs had `outline: none` with a 2.3:1 border as the only focus cue. | `useModalFocus`; one focus ring everywhere, inputs get a ring + tinted halo. |
| 13 | Toasts never expired, stacked unbounded, no clear-all; errors announced politely. | 6 s auto-dismiss for `ok`, errors persist as `role="alert"`, scrolling stack, Clear all. |
| 14 | Bin tile: 9px mono label over the image, truncated to ~11 chars; ✕ always visible; no hover; no kind badge for sound/video legible. | Caption under the thumbnail (11px, middle-truncated at 18 chars), ✕ on hover/focus, hover lift, AUDIO/VIDEO badge. |
| 15 | Missing media: clip drew a broken image (only `!asset` counted as offline); inspector said nothing. | Offline = missing or no src on track and lanes; bin tile hatches with OFFLINE; inspector shows a callout. |
| 16 | Inspector was text-only. | Card heads carry a thumbnail; the cut card shows both frames; a transition's From/To select the source clip when it is still on the track. |
| 17 | "Animate all" wore the lit style while disabled; no `:disabled` style for tools/transport at all. | Lit only when it can run; tools and transport dim at 0.4. |
| 18 | "1 clips"; four empty states repeating each other; `.linklike` wrapping its icon; off-grid paddings (5/7/9/11/13/15). | Pluralised; empty states each say one thing; spacing on the 4px scale with named tokens. |

## Deliberately not done (worth their own tickets)

- **Undo.** Delete is still one-way. A recoverable-delete toast ("Deleted X — Undo") would cover
  most of the value without a history stack.
- **Right-click menu / Duplicate.** Native context menus are suppressed on clips and lanes;
  nothing replaces them yet.
- **Scroll-to-zoom and fit-to-window.** Zoom is the slider plus a 100% reset.
- **Film wizard entry point.** Still none, per README.
- **Verify on the packaged app.** Overlay title bar geometry and WKWebView container-query
  units were checked against the Tauri 2.11 schema and Safari 16+ support, not on a build.
